FAQ

Does CopyPilot change variants or stock?

No. Publish updates title, description, SEO metafields, and image alt text only.

How do credits work?

1 generation = 1 credit. Regenerating spends again. Bulk consumes one credit per product processed.

Is there a blog writer?

Not in the current codebase. CopyPilot focuses on product listing SEO & sales copy in Admin.
